Privacy Policy

Last updated · 2026-07-23

Spruce is a Mac disk-cleaning utility that runs entirely on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Spruce does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. It has no user accounts, no analytics or telemetry, no advertising, and no crash-reporting service.

Network access

Spruce never connects to the internet. It has no networking code and makes no outbound requests of any kind. Everything it does — scanning, sizing, cleaning, uninstalling — happens locally on your Mac.

File access

To measure and clean reclaimable space, Spruce asks your permission to access your home folder using the standard macOS file picker (a requirement of the App Sandbox). This access is used only on your device to display and remove files you select. Files are moved to the Trash and can be recovered, unless you explicitly choose “Delete Permanently.” Spruce never uploads, copies, or reads your files off your device.

Data storage

Spruce stores a small amount of information locally on your Mac — such as your scan preferences, lifetime-freed total, and an operation log of what was cleaned — inside its own sandbox container. This never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Children's privacy

Spruce does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new “last updated” date.
