Calendar Spam Cleaner 支持

面向 Calendar Spam Cleaner iOS 应用的支持页面

Calendar Spam Cleaner 是一个本机日历清理工具，用于扫描疑似垃圾事件、重复事件和订阅日历相关项目，并帮助你在确认后删除可编辑事件。

常见问题

  • 如果应用无法扫描日历，请在 iOS 设置中确认 Calendar Spam Cleaner 已获得日历访问权限。
  • 如果某些事件无法删除，通常是因为对应日历为只读、订阅日历或系统不允许第三方应用修改。
  • 订阅日历通常需要在 iOS 设置中移除：设置 → 日历 → 账户。
  • 扫描完成后，可打开“我的垃圾日历来自哪里？”查看订阅来源、重复、可疑 URL 和异常频率等本机诊断信号。
  • Calendar Spam Cleaner Pro 购买和恢复由 Apple App Store 处理。你可以在应用内使用“恢复购买”。

隐私

Calendar Spam Cleaner 在设备本机处理日历扫描和来源诊断，不要求账号，也不上传你的日历数据或诊断结果。详情请查看 隐私政策。

联系开发者

Calendar Spam Cleaner 由个人开发者维护。如需反馈问题、请求支持或咨询购买相关问题，请通过 App Store 产品页显示的开发者联系方式联系开发者。反馈时建议包含你的 iOS 版本、应用版本、问题出现的步骤和相关截图。

Calendar Spam Cleaner Support

Support page for the Calendar Spam Cleaner iOS app

Calendar Spam Cleaner is an on-device calendar cleanup tool that scans for suspicious events, duplicate events, and subscribed-calendar items, then helps you remove editable events after confirmation.

FAQ

  • If the app cannot scan your calendar, check iOS Settings and make sure Calendar Spam Cleaner has calendar access.
  • If some events cannot be deleted, the calendar may be read-only, subscribed, or not editable by third-party apps.
  • Subscribed calendars usually need to be removed in iOS Settings: Settings → Calendar → Accounts.
  • After a scan, open "Where did my calendar spam come from?" to review on-device signals such as subscription sources, duplicates, suspicious URLs, and unusual frequency.
  • Calendar Spam Cleaner Pro purchases and restores are handled by Apple App Store. You can use Restore Purchases inside the app.

Privacy

Calendar Spam Cleaner processes calendar scanning and source diagnosis on your device. It does not require an account and does not upload calendar data or diagnostic results. See the Privacy Policy for details.
