IDMint: Passport Photo Maker Support

Help for the IDMint: Passport Photo Maker iPhone app

For support, email wanglingbo0227@126.com. Please include your app version, iOS version, and device model. Do not send sensitive photos or personal information.

Why can’t I save a photo?

In iOS Settings, find Passport Photo and allow photo access. To save an export, the app needs permission to add photos.

Why does automatic positioning not work?

Use a well-lit, front-facing portrait with a simple background. You can always adjust the crop manually if a face is not detected.

Where are my photos processed?

Photo processing happens on your device. The app does not upload photos or require an account.

Privacy Policy

IDMint 证件照制作 支持

如需帮助，请发送邮件至 wanglingbo0227@126.com。请附上应用版本、iOS 版本和设备型号，不要发送敏感照片或个人信息。

为什么无法保存照片？

请在 iOS“设置”中找到“证件照制作”，并允许照片访问。保存成品需要允许本应用添加照片。

为什么自动构图没有生效？

请使用光线充足、正面且背景简单的照片。如果未检测到人脸，也可以手动调整裁切位置。

照片在哪里处理？

照片始终在你的设备上处理。本应用不会上传照片，也不需要账号。
