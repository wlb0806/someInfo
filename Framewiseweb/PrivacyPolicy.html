Privacy Policy

Framewise Photo Studio is designed to prepare and export photos directly on your device.

Effective date: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Framewise Photo Studio (the “App”) handles information when you use the iOS App. The App does not require an account and does not send your photos to a server operated by the publisher.

Information We Process

The App processes the photos and editing choices that you provide so it can crop, size, arrange, and export a finished image. This processing takes place locally on your device. We do not collect names, email addresses, contact lists, precise location, identifiers for advertising, or biometric profiles through the App.

Camera and Photo Library

If you use the camera, the App accesses the camera to capture an original photo. If you import a photo, the App accesses the item you choose from your Photo Library. If you save an export, the App uses the permission you grant to add that file to your Photo Library. iOS controls these permissions, and you can change them at any time in Settings.

On-Device Editing

Editing, image analysis, sizing, layout, and export are performed on your device. The App does not upload photos, edits, or document contents to the publisher, an analytics provider, or an advertising network.

Sharing and Export

When you choose to share or export a file, iOS presents the system share sheet or a destination you select. The selected recipient or service may receive the file and handle it under its own privacy policy. The App does not control that recipient’s practices.

Data Retention and Deletion

The App does not maintain a publisher-controlled account or remote database for your content. Temporary files created for export are stored in the App’s local temporary storage and may be removed by iOS when space is needed. Photos saved to your Photo Library remain there until you delete them using Apple’s Photos app.

Third-Party Services

The App uses Apple-provided iOS features such as the camera, Photo Library, Vision, Core Image, and the system share sheet. These features are governed by Apple’s terms and privacy practices. The App does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App does not require an account or transmit content to the publisher, it is not designed to collect personal information from any user.

Security

Keeping photos on your device reduces the information sent outside the App, but no software or device can guarantee absolute security. Use the device security features and permissions provided by iOS.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App or its data practices change. The updated version will include a new effective date and will be published at this URL.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the publisher through the support contact or support URL listed for Framewise Photo Studio on its App Store product page.
