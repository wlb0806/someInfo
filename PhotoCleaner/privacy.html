Privacy Policy

Your photos stay yours.

Yingxu is an iPhone app for organizing and cleaning up photos and videos. Core scanning and analysis happen on your device. We do not upload your photos or videos to our own servers.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Yingxu iOS app. “Yingxu,” “we,” or “us” means the publisher of the app. Apple’s services, including Photos, iCloud, StoreKit, and the system share sheet, are governed by Apple’s own policies and terms.

2. Photos access

When you choose “Allow Full Access” in the iOS permission prompt, Yingxu may read photos and videos in your photo library and necessary system information, such as asset identifiers, creation dates, dimensions, format, duration, file size, favorite or edited status, and system categories such as screenshots or screen recordings.

If you grant only limited access, Yingxu cannot scan your complete library and may ask you to update the permission in Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos. Yingxu does not access your contacts, location, microphone, or camera for its cleaning features.

3. How we use your content

  • Identify exact duplicates, similar photos, screenshots, large videos, and other items you may want to organize;
  • Generate scan results, groups, thumbnails, and cleanup suggestions for your review;
  • At your direction, ask the system Photos library to move selected items to Recently Deleted; and
  • Remember your keep choices, cleanup history, and in-app usage allowance on your device.

Image analysis is performed on your device and is not uploaded to our servers. For items stored only in iCloud, Yingxu uses Apple’s Photos framework to request the resources or thumbnails needed for the feature. Those requests are subject to Apple’s iCloud and Photos services.

4. Information stored on your device

Yingxu may store photo asset identifiers, numerical analysis results, grouping results, your keep choices, cleanup history, and app settings locally on your device. This information is used to restore scan results and provide app features. We do not upload it or use it to build a personal profile.

Yingxu does not require an account, use advertising, or include analytics tools intended for cross-app tracking. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

5. Purchases and sharing

If you purchase a permanent unlock, payment, transaction processing, and purchase restoration are handled by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. We do not receive your card number, payment password, or complete payment details. Purchase status is based on the transaction result returned by Apple.

When you tap a share button, iOS opens the system share sheet. You decide what to share, with whom, and whether to send it. Yingxu does not share photos or personal information automatically.

6. Deletion and your controls

Yingxu requests deletion only after you select items and confirm cleanup. By default, selected items are moved to iOS Recently Deleted, where you decide whether to permanently erase them. You can revoke or change Yingxu’s Photos permission at any time in iOS Settings.

Important: Yingxu does not silently delete photos. Review the selected items and confirm the cleanup before the system Photos library is changed.

7. Third-party services

Yingxu uses Apple’s Photos framework, iCloud resource access, App Store / StoreKit, and the system share sheet. These services are provided by Apple under Apple’s own privacy policy and terms. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

8. Children’s privacy

Yingxu is not directed specifically to children. We do not knowingly collect a child’s name, contact information, or other online identity information. Photo content remains under the device owner’s system permissions and in-app controls.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Yingxu’s features or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a revised policy is published. Continued use of Yingxu after an update means that you have reviewed the revised policy.

10. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or privacy-related matters, email wanglingbo@runzeyuan.com.

影序隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 23 日

影序（英文名：Yingxu，以下简称“本应用”）是一款用于整理和清理 iPhone 照片与视频的工具。本应用的核心扫描和分析功能在你的设备上完成，我们不会把你的照片或视频上传到自己的服务器。

我们访问哪些内容

当你允许“完全访问”照片后，本应用可能读取照片图库中的照片、视频及必要的系统信息，例如资源标识、创建时间、尺寸、格式、时长、文件大小、收藏或编辑状态，以及截图、录屏等系统分类信息。若只允许“限制访问”，本应用无法扫描完整图库。我们不会为清理功能读取通讯录、位置、麦克风或相机。

我们如何使用这些内容

本应用在本机识别重复和相似照片、截图、大视频等项目，生成扫描结果、分组、缩略图和清理建议，并在你明确确认后请求系统照片库将所选项目移入“最近删除”。照片和视频的图像分析不会上传到我们的服务器。

我们保存什么

本应用可能在你的设备本地保存照片资源标识、分析结果、分组结果、保留选择、清理历史和应用设置，用于恢复扫描结果和提供功能。我们不上传这些信息，也不建立个人画像。本应用不要求注册账号、不使用广告，也不集成跨应用跟踪分析工具。

购买、分享和删除

永久解锁的付款、交易处理和恢复由 Apple App Store / StoreKit 完成。点击分享按钮后，系统会打开 iOS 分享面板，分享对象和内容由你决定。只有在你选择项目并确认清理后，本应用才会请求系统照片库执行删除；默认会进入“最近删除”。

第三方服务、儿童隐私和更新

本应用使用 Apple 的照片框架、iCloud 资源访问、App Store / StoreKit 和系统分享面板，这些服务适用 Apple 自己的隐私政策和服务条款。我们不会主动收集儿童的姓名、联系方式或其他在线身份信息。如功能或法律要求变化，我们可能更新本政策并更新生效日期。
