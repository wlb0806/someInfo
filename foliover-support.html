Getting started

  • Photo cleanup: open Home → Photo cleanup. Grant photo access, run a review, and inspect a category. Select items and confirm removal. Removed photos can be recovered in Photos → Recently Deleted.
  • Timestamp camera: open Home → Timestamp camera. Capture or import a photo, choose the stamp, and save a new copy or keep it in Your work. Location is optional.
  • Portrait background: open Home → Portrait background. Choose a preset or custom background color. Import from Photos, Camera or Files. Crop and choose a size. Enable Print sheet for multiple copies, select paper, preview pages, and export.
  • Image compression: open Home → Image compression. Import from Photos, Camera or Files. Choose a size limit, resize or convert, and generate a new copy. Completed outputs stay in Your work. Adjustments to the same source are included in its use.
  • Calendar cleanup: open Home → Calendar cleanup. Choose a review window, grant access, review each event and confirm cleanup. Calendar deletions cannot be undone in Foliover. Read-only calendar subscriptions may need to be removed in Apple's Calendar app.

Purchases and free use

Each service includes one free successful use. Afterwards, optionally complete a share of the app for one additional use of that service, or make a one-time purchase for lifetime access. Each cleanup batch counts as one use. Failed or cancelled operations do not use an allowance.

All Services Lifetime includes Calendar cleanup, Photo cleanup, Timestamp camera, Portrait background (including sizing and print layouts), and Image compression. US prices: $2.99 for all services or $0.99 per service. The price and currency shown by your App Store apply. Open You → Foliover Plus → Restore purchases using the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Sharing grants one extra use after a completed share; opening or cancelling the share sheet does not grant access. The app does not read recipients. Local free-use records are stored on the device; purchases are restored through Apple.

Permissions and exports

Check iOS Settings → Apps → Foliover if photo, calendar or camera access is disabled. Permission is requested only for the feature you choose. Your work keeps exported files on your device, where you can preview, share or delete them. Deleting a Your work export does not remove copies already saved elsewhere.

Privacy

Photo and calendar analysis, editing and stamp rendering run on your device. No app account is required.
