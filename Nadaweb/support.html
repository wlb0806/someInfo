01 / DATA

Your records are private

No account, bank connection, advertising, analytics, or tracking. Your challenge data stays on your device unless you turn on private iCloud sync.

02 / SYNC

iCloud sync is optional

Dewday can sync through your private CloudKit database. Turn it on from the Settings tab, then restart Dewday for the change to take effect.

Frequently asked

Good to know.

Which languages does Dewday 1.1 support?

English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, French, and German. Dewday follows your preferred supported language. You can also choose Dewday’s language in iOS Settings when the app-language option is available. Reopen Dewday after changing it so the widget and existing reminders update too.

Is the money-kept total my bank balance?

No. It is an estimate based on your chosen daily baseline and the purchases you record as resisted. Dewday does not connect to a bank, transfer money, or convert currencies. Changing the app’s language changes the number format, not the currency or stored amounts.

简体中文：如何切换语言？

Dewday 1.1 支持英语、简繁中文、日语、韩语、法语和德语，并优先使用你的系统语言。也可以在 iOS 设置中选择 Dewday 的语言（如有此选项），重新打开 App 后，小组件和现有提醒会一起更新。金额是参考估算，不是银行余额；切换语言不会换算货币。

繁體中文：如何切換語言？

Dewday 1.1 支援英語、簡繁中文、日語、韓語、法語和德語，並優先使用你的系統語言。也可以在 iOS 設定中選擇 Dewday 的語言（如有此選項），重新開啟 App 後，小工具和現有提醒會一起更新。金額是參考估算，不是銀行餘額；切換語言不會換算貨幣。

日本語：言語を変更するには？

Dewday 1.1は英語、簡体字・繁体字中国語、日本語、韓国語、フランス語、ドイツ語に対応しています。端末の優先言語に従います。iOSの設定でDewdayの言語を選べる場合は、変更後にアプリを再起動してください。ウィジェットと既存の通知も更新されます。表示金額は推定値で、銀行残高ではありません。言語変更による通貨換算は行いません。

한국어: 언어는 어떻게 바꾸나요?

Dewday 1.1은 영어, 중국어 간체·번체, 일본어, 한국어, 프랑스어, 독일어를 지원하며 기기의 우선 언어를 따릅니다. iOS 설정에 Dewday 언어 옵션이 있으면 변경한 후 앱을 다시 실행해 주세요. 위젯과 기존 알림도 업데이트돼요. 표시 금액은 추정치이며 은행 잔액이 아니에요. 언어를 바꿔도 통화를 환산하지 않아요.

Si l’option est disponible, choisissez la langue de Dewday dans les réglages iOS, puis rouvrez l’app pour actualiser le widget et les rappels existants. Deutsch: Wie ändere ich die Sprache?

Dewday 1.1 folgt deiner bevorzugten unterstützten Sprache: Englisch, vereinfachtes oder traditionelles Chinesisch, Japanisch, Koreanisch, Französisch oder Deutsch. Falls verfügbar, wähle die Sprache für Dewday in den iOS-Einstellungen und öffne die App erneut. So werden auch Widget und bestehende Erinnerungen aktualisiert. Beträge sind Schätzwerte, kein Bankkontostand. Ein Sprachwechsel rechnet keine Währungen um.

How do the daily savings challenges work?

Open the Challenges tab and choose 7 days, one calendar month, or one calendar year. Set a daily amount and an optional reward you arrange for yourself. Record today or catch up on past dates in your plan; future dates are unavailable. You can correct mistaken records. Months and years use their actual calendar lengths.

Each recorded day earns growth once, and completing a plan earns a bonus and a badge. Repeatedly undoing and recording a date does not earn extra growth. Points and badges are virtual; Dewday does not pay money or provide prizes. Your personal reward is separate from the app.

Where are settings and legal information?

Use the Settings tab to edit your daily reference amount, change appearance, manage private iCloud sync, or read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

Do I need an account?

No. Dewday has no sign-up and no developer-operated account system. You can start a no-spend streak immediately after onboarding.

Do I need a subscription to keep using Dewday?

Starting with Dewday 1.1.1, daily check-ins, history, and savings challenges are free for everyone. You can continue from the records you already have. Sharing a progress card or no-buy receipt is optional.

Where is my data stored?

By default, your streaks, challenges, resisted purchases, and preferences are stored locally on your device. If you explicitly enable iCloud sync, compatible records are also stored in your private iCloud database.

How do I turn iCloud sync on or off?

Open the Settings tab and choose “iCloud sync.” Restart the app after changing it. Turning sync off stops future syncing but does not automatically erase existing data from iCloud.

Why is the widget not up to date?

Open Dewday once and record or edit a check-in. iOS controls widget refresh timing, so updates may not appear instantly. If needed, remove the widget and add it again.

How do I delete my data?

Because Dewday has no developer account, there is no server account to delete. Remove local records from the app where available, or delete the app to remove its local container. If you enabled iCloud sync, you can manage Dewday’s stored data from your Apple Account’s iCloud storage settings.
