LightPress: Photo Compressor
隐私政策

本政策适用于 LightPress: Photo Compressor iOS 应用（以下简称“LightPress”或“本应用”，设备桌面显示为“LightPress”，中文品牌名称为“轻压图”）。开发者及个人信息处理者：lingbo wang（以下简称“我们”）。本政策说明应用内的图片处理，以及你主动联系开发者时的信息处理方式。

1. 我们处理哪些信息，以及为什么处理

为了完成你选择的任务，本应用会在设备本地读取和处理你通过系统照片选择器、文件选择器导入的图片，或你使用相机拍摄的照片。处理内容包括图片像素、文件名、文件大小、格式、宽高、方向及必要的色彩信息；你设置的压缩上限、画质、尺寸和裁剪位置用于生成结果。

这些图片和处理参数不会由本应用发送至开发者服务器。本应用不要求注册，不建立个人账号或云端图片库，不集成广告、第三方统计或追踪 SDK，也不主动采集广告标识符、精确位置、联系人、麦克风录音或跨应用浏览记录。

图片可能包含人脸、证件、文字或其他个人信息。本应用仅按你的操作对图片进行处理，不实施人脸身份识别，不提取生物识别模板，不将图片内容用于广告或 AI 模型训练。

如果你通过邮件联系我们，我们会收到你主动发送的邮箱地址、邮件内容及附件，仅用于回复、排查问题和处理你的请求。请尽量只提供解决问题所需的信息；通常无需发送原始照片或证件。

2. 系统权限与选择器

权限仅用于你主动选择的功能
功能使用方式与目的拒绝或取消后的影响
从照片选择通过 iOS 系统照片选择器读取你选中的图片，不申请整个照片库的读取权限。取消选择后不导入图片；仍可从文件选择或拍照。
拍照你点击“拍照”时才申请相机权限，用于拍摄待处理照片。无法拍照；仍可处理从照片或文件导入的图片。
保存到照片你选择保存时才申请向照片库添加图片的权限，用于保存处理后图片的新副本。无法保存到照片；仍可保存到文件或分享。
从文件选择／保存到文件通过系统文件选择器访问你指定的文件或保存位置。文件提供商可能下载云端原文件。取消后不完成此次导入或导出。

你可以在 iPhone 的“设置”中找到 LightPress 或“隐私与安全性”，查看并更改系统允许管理的权限。授权相机或照片写入权限，不代表授权我们将图片上传、公开或用于其他目的。

3. 图片保存期限与临时文件清理

为了读取、预览、处理和导出，应用会在自身临时目录内保存来源副本及输出文件。当前任务期间，这些文件会保留，以便检查结果、重新调整或导出。取消处理后，当前来源副本可能保留，以便重试。

  • 更换图片时，应用会清理旧来源副本及已失效的输出文件。
  • 返回首页或选择“处理下一张”时，会清理当前任务的临时来源与结果。
  • 修改处理要求时，已生成的旧结果会失效并被清理。
  • 你可在应用“设置”中选择“清除临时文件”，结束当前任务并清理临时副本。
  • 应用再次启动时，会清理上次任务遗留的应用临时文件；iOS 也可能根据系统规则回收临时数据。

清理操作不删除原始照片，也不删除你已经保存到照片库、文件或其他应用的导出副本。你可以通过对应的系统服务删除已保存的文件，并按需要管理“最近删除”、云端同步或备份中的副本。

你主动发送的支持邮件，仅在回复、跟进及解决请求所需期间保留；请求结束后，我们会删除不再需要的信息。法律要求保留或尚未结束的争议处理所需记录除外。

4. 图片元数据与隐私保护

来源文件可能包含位置、设备型号或拍摄时间等元数据，临时来源副本也可能保留这些信息。生成输出时，应用重新编码图片，默认不复制来源文件中的 GPS、相机设备及拍摄日期等个人元数据；为正确显示图片，可能写入方向、尺寸或色彩等必要信息。

元数据处理不会移除画面里可见的人脸、地址、证件号码或其他个人信息。分享前，请自行检查图片内容和接收对象。

5. 保存、分享与第三方服务

只有在你选择“保存到照片”“保存到文件”或“分享”时，应用才会把输出文件交给你指定的系统服务、文件提供商或接收应用。我们不出售图片或个人信息，不向广告商、数据经纪商提供图片，也不建立面向第三方的图片上传服务。

从 iCloud 照片或云端文件提供商选择图片时，系统或相应服务可能需要联网下载原图。保存到 iCloud、使用云端文件提供商或分享给其他应用时，相应服务可能同步、传输或再次处理图片。上述服务依据其自身的隐私政策和你的设置运行，包括其数据存储地点与保存期限；你可以选择设备本地文件作为导出位置。

本应用的图片处理不向开发者服务器传输图片，也不由开发者发起图片的跨境传输。你主动选择的外部服务及邮件服务可能具有不同的数据处理安排。

Apple 可能根据你的系统设置处理系统诊断、商店或 iCloud 服务信息。这些系统行为适用 Apple 隐私政策；应用未集成额外的诊断上报 SDK。

6. 你的选择与个人信息权利

你可以自主选择要处理的图片、取消操作、撤回系统权限、清理临时文件以及删除已导出的副本。你可以卸载应用，清理由系统管理的应用本地数据；照片库、外部文件、云端和备份中的副本需在相应服务中另行管理。

对于你主动通过邮件提供给我们的个人信息，你可通过本政策中的邮箱请求查阅、复制、更正、删除、撤回同意或获得处理方式的解释。我们会在确认请求与相关信息的关系后，按适用法律处理，不要求你提供与请求无关的个人信息。

由于图片和任务参数由应用在你的设备上处理，我们没有这些图片的服务器副本，无法从开发者服务器检索或恢复它们。拒绝某项可选权限只影响相应功能，不妨碍其他不依赖该权限的功能。

7. 信息安全

本应用利用 iOS 应用沙盒、系统权限及用户触发的选择器限制文件访问，并减少图片副本的保留。请妥善管理设备访问权限、设备备份及接收图片的应用。通过邮件提供的信息会仅用于处理相应请求，并限制不必要的访问和保留。

8. 未成年人

本应用是通用图片处理工具，不以识别用户年龄或建立未成年人档案为目的。未成年人应在监护人指导下使用；不满 14 周岁的用户应在监护人知情同意下使用涉及个人信息的功能，避免导入或分享不必要的敏感图片。

如果监护人认为未成年人在未获适当同意的情况下向开发者提供了个人信息，可通过下方邮箱联系我们，请求核查并删除相应信息。

9. 本隐私政策网页

本页面自身不包含广告、访问统计脚本、追踪像素、外部字体或 Cookie 代码。访问部署后的页面仍会与托管服务器建立网络连接；托管服务可能根据其配置记录 IP 地址、访问时间、请求路径等日志。此类网页访问记录的实际处理方式取决于网站托管服务，不同于应用内的图片本地处理。

点击外部服务链接或发送邮件时，相关浏览器、邮件服务及目标网站会按其规则处理相应请求。

10. 政策更新

我们可能因功能或适用要求的变化更新本政策，并修改页面上的更新日期。若图片上传、第三方 SDK、信息种类、目的或处理方式发生实质变化，我们会在相关功能上线前以适当方式说明，并在依法需要时取得新的授权或同意。政策更新不会代替相机等系统权限授权。

LightPress: Photo Compressor
Privacy Policy

Updated and effective October 1, 2026 · Policy version 1.0

This policy applies to the LightPress: Photo Compressor iOS app (“LightPress” or “the app”). Its home screen display name is “LightPress,” and its Chinese brand name is “轻压图.” The developer and data controller is lingbo wang (“we”). It describes image processing in the app and information you voluntarily send when contacting us.

1. Information processed and its purpose

The app processes images you select through the system Photos or Files picker, or take with the camera, on your device. Image pixels, file names, sizes, formats, dimensions, orientation, necessary color information and your processing settings are used to preview, compress, resize, crop, convert and export images.

The app does not send your images or processing settings to developer-operated servers. It does not require an account, maintain a cloud image library, integrate advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, or actively collect advertising identifiers, precise location, contacts, microphone recordings or cross-app browsing history.

Images may contain personal or sensitive information. The app does not identify people through their faces, extract biometric identity templates, or use images for advertising or AI model training.

If you email us, we receive the address, message and attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, investigate issues and handle your request. Please send only what is needed; original photos or identity documents are usually unnecessary.

2. Permissions and system pickers

  • Choose from Photos: the system picker provides only the image you select. The app does not request read access to your entire photo library.
  • Camera: requested when you choose Take a Photo, to capture an image for processing. If denied, you can still import from Photos or Files.
  • Save to Photos: add-only photo permission is requested when you save a new processed copy. If denied, you can still save to Files or share.
  • Files: the system picker accesses the file or destination you choose. A file provider may download a cloud file. Cancelling stops that import or export.

You can manage available permissions in iPhone Settings. Permission to use the camera or add a photo does not authorize us to upload, publish or use images for unrelated purposes.

3. Retention and deletion

Source copies and generated output are kept in the app’s temporary directory for the current task, preview, retry and export. A cancelled operation may retain the current source for retry. Replacing the image, returning home or starting the next task removes obsolete task copies; changing requirements removes the previous output. Settings → Clear temporary files clears the current task. On the next app launch, the app clears its previous temporary files. iOS may also reclaim temporary data.

This cleanup does not delete your original images or copies exported to Photos, Files or other apps. Manage those copies, Recently Deleted items, cloud copies and backups through their respective services.

Support emails are kept only as needed to respond to, follow up and resolve your request. Information no longer needed is deleted after the request is resolved, except records required by applicable law or an ongoing dispute.

4. Image metadata

Source files and temporary source copies may contain location, camera or capture-date metadata. When generating output, the app re-encodes the image and, by default, does not copy original GPS, camera-device or capture-date metadata. Necessary orientation, dimensions or color information may be written for correct display.

This does not remove personal information visible in the image, such as faces, addresses or document numbers. Review the image and recipient before sharing.

5. Export, sharing and third-party services

Only when you choose Save to Photos, Save to Files or Share does the app hand output to the system service, file provider or recipient app you select. We do not sell your images or personal information, provide images to advertisers or data brokers, or operate an image-upload service.

Selecting an iCloud photo or cloud-provider file may cause that service to download it. Exporting to a cloud destination or another app may result in synchronization, transfer or further processing under that service’s privacy policy and your settings, including its storage locations and retention periods. You may choose a local device destination.

Image processing in the app does not transmit images to developer-operated servers or initiate developer-operated cross-border image transfers. Services you choose, including email providers, may have different processing arrangements.

Apple may process system diagnostics, App Store or iCloud service information according to your settings and Apple’s Privacy Policy. The app integrates no additional diagnostic reporting SDK.

6. Your choices and rights

You may choose images, cancel operations, revoke permissions, clear temporary files and delete exported copies. Uninstalling removes app data managed by the system; exported files, cloud copies and backups must be managed separately.

For personal information you voluntarily send us by email, contact us to request access, a copy, correction, deletion, withdrawal of consent or an explanation of processing. We handle requests under applicable law after verifying their connection to the relevant information, without requesting unrelated personal data.

We have no server copies of images processed on your device and cannot retrieve or restore them from a developer server. Declining an optional permission affects only the feature that needs it.

7. Security

The app uses iOS sandboxing, permissions and user-initiated pickers to limit file access and reduces retention of image copies. Manage access to your device, its backups and recipient apps carefully. Support information is used only to handle the request, with unnecessary access and retention restricted.

8. Children

LightPress is a general-purpose image tool and does not seek to identify users’ ages or build child profiles. Minors should use it with guidance from a parent or guardian. Users under 14 should have a guardian’s informed consent for features involving personal information and avoid importing or sharing unnecessary sensitive images.

A guardian who believes a child sent us personal information without appropriate consent may contact us to request investigation and deletion.

9. This policy webpage

This page contains no advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, external fonts or cookie code. Accessing the hosted page still connects your browser to its host, which may log IP addresses, access times and request paths according to its configuration. Actual handling depends on the hosting service and is separate from on-device image processing in the app. External links and emails are handled by their respective websites and services.

10. Changes

We may update this policy as features or applicable requirements change and revise the date above. Before introducing material changes to image uploading, SDKs, data categories, purposes or processing, we will provide appropriate notice and obtain new authorization or consent where required. Policy updates do not replace system permission requests.
