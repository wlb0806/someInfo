Privacy Policy

How the current version of the Stayframe app handles your rental records.

01About this policy

Stayframe helps you document rented-home conditions, compare move-in and move-out records, and prepare reports. The developer is Lingbo Wang. This policy describes the current version of Stayframe.

02Your records and storage

House names and addresses, space details, photos, videos, notes, record history, communication attachments and saved reports are stored locally on your device. Stayframe does not require an account, upload these records to a developer-operated server, or currently provide iCloud sync. Device backups may include app data depending on your Apple settings; Apple handles those backups under its own terms.

03Permissions and reminders

Camera, photo-library and microphone permissions are used when you choose to capture or add photos or videos. Notification permission is used for local reminders about confirming that you sent a move-in report. You can manage these permissions in iOS Settings.

04Exporting and sharing

Reports and files are exported or shared only when you choose an export or share action and a destination. Recipients and services you choose handle those copies under their own terms. Opening the EULA loads Apple's website in an in-app browser.

05Tracking and analytics

The current version does not include third-party advertising, tracking SDKs or developer analytics. The developer does not receive your rental records through the app.

06Deleting records and keeping copies

You can delete records and houses in the app. Archiving retains records; it does not delete them. Deletion does not remove copies you have already exported or shared, or existing device backups. Keep independent copies of important records. Removing the app removes its local data, subject to iOS backup and restore behavior.

07Contact and updates

For privacy questions, contact wanglingbo0227@126.com. This policy may be updated when the app changes. The effective date appears above.

01关于本政策

Stayframe 帮助你记录租住房屋的状况、对照入住与退房记录，以及整理报告。开发者为 Lingbo Wang。本政策说明当前版本的数据处理方式。

02记录与存储

房屋名称与地址、空间信息、照片、视频、备注、修改历史、沟通附件及已保存的报告均存储在你的设备本地。Stayframe 不要求注册账户，不将这些记录上传至开发者运营的服务器，当前版本也不提供 iCloud 同步。根据你的 Apple 设置，设备备份可能包含应用数据；此类备份由 Apple 按其条款处理。

03权限与提醒

仅在你选择拍摄或添加照片、视频时使用相机、相册与麦克风权限。通知权限用于在设备本地提醒你确认入住报告是否已发送。你可以在 iOS 设置中管理这些权限。

04导出与分享

仅在你主动选择导出或分享操作及目标位置时导出或分享报告与文件。你选择的接收方和服务按各自条款处理这些副本。打开 EULA 时，应用内浏览器会访问 Apple 网站。

05跟踪与分析

当前版本不包含第三方广告、跟踪 SDK 或开发者分析工具。开发者不会通过应用接收你的租住记录。

06删除记录与保留副本

你可以在应用内删除记录和房屋。归档会保留记录，并不等于删除。删除不会移除你已导出、已分享的副本或已有设备备份。请为重要记录另行保留副本。删除应用会移除其本地数据，具体受 iOS 备份与恢复机制影响。

07联系与更新

如有隐私问题，请联系 wanglingbo0227@126.com。应用功能变化时，本政策可能随之更新。生效日期见本页上方。
