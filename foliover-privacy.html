Foliover is provided by LingBo wang, an individual developer based in China (“we,” “us,” or “our”). For personal information we receive through support or privacy requests, LingBo wang is responsible for the processing described in this policy. Contact: wanglingbo0227@126.com.

This policy covers the Foliover iOS app, including photo review, calendar care, Timestamp, Photo & print, and related support communications. It describes the current app, not the separate policies of earlier independent apps or third-party services.

02Privacy at a glance

Foliover does not require an account. Photo and calendar analysis, photo editing, background processing and stamp rendering run on your device. The app does not send your photo library, calendar contents, review results or location history to a developer-operated server.

We do not include advertising or third-party tracking or analytics SDKs, request cross-app tracking permission, sell personal information, or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not use your photos, calendar or location to train a developer-operated AI model.

Local processing does not mean every optional action is offline. Apple handles purchases and optional address lookup; system pickers, backups, exports and communications can involve the providers you use, as explained below.

03Photos, imports and local analysis

When you start a photo review, Foliover requests photo-library access. With limited access, it reviews only the items iOS makes available under your selection. The review uses image or video content and metadata such as library identifiers, creation and modification dates, dimensions, duration, estimated size, favorites and edit status.

The app computes local fingerprints, similarity groups and quality observations to help you review duplicate, similar or other potentially unwanted items. Review metadata, fingerprints, categories and detection reasons may be cached inside the app. These are suggestions; you choose items and confirm before deletion.

Photo & print may detect face position and pose to provide composition tips, and use on-device image processing to separate a person from the background. It does not identify people, compare identities, create an identity database, or send face measurements to us. Working analysis is used for the current edit, not retained by us as a biometric identity profile.

Importing an individual photo uses the system Photos or Files picker without requiring full-library permission. The selected file may contain capture dates, time-zone information and GPS metadata, which Timestamp can read to display the photo’s own context. Apple or your file provider may download a selected cloud file; that transfer is separate from local analysis.

04Calendar care

Calendar permission is requested when you start a calendar review. The app locally reads available events and calendar information, including titles, dates, notes, locations, URLs and calendar or source labels, to identify possible duplicates or unwanted events. It does not upload these contents to us or visit event URLs as part of the scan.

Scanning does not automatically change your calendar. Changes apply only to the items you select and confirm, subject to calendar permissions and editability. Your calendar provider may synchronize changes to your other devices. Read-only subscriptions may need to be managed through iOS Settings. Foliover has no in-app recovery service for deleted calendar events.

05Camera, time and optional location

Camera access is requested when you enable capture. Camera frames and captured photos are processed locally. Foliover does not request microphone permission for its still-photo capture feature.

Live location is off by default. If you enable it, iOS location permission is used while the feature is active to obtain coordinates, accuracy and a capture time for a stamp. The app does not request background location access or operate a developer location-history service. You can use approximate location where iOS allows it, a custom label, or no location.

When you choose device location, Foliover automatically sends its coordinates to Apple’s geocoding service to obtain a full address; there is no separate address-lookup switch. After an address is resolved, location updates and automatic address queries stop, and the saved location remains until you refresh or replace it. The app keeps the original sample time rather than treating a saved point as a new GPS fix. You can edit the address text without changing its coordinates; the stamp marks edited text accordingly. For an imported photo, opening its location editor can resolve that photo’s existing coordinates without using your current GPS position. Apple handles its service under its own privacy terms. Choose a custom label or no location if you do not want an address lookup.

Timestamp exports are new image copies that omit source GPS and private source EXIF metadata. Any time, place, coordinates or note drawn visibly into the stamp remains part of the image when you share it. Stamps describe device time, photo metadata or your edits; they do not certify authenticity or prevent later editing.

06Settings, files and access records

Foliover stores preferences such as units, introduction completion, stamp layout, saved custom labels and notes, and local cleanup counts on the device. Studio photo and print exports, thumbnails and project descriptions are stored in the app so you can reopen, save or share them. Stamped photos are kept in Studio only when you choose Save to Studio. Imported and rendered working files may also be held in temporary storage.

Paid access uses verified Apple transaction information. The app stores each service’s used free trial and unspent share-use credit in iOS Keychain. Legacy calendar usage records may also remain for migration. These records are not uploaded to us, are not synchronized through a developer account, and may survive deleting and reinstalling the app depending on iOS behavior.

When you choose to share the app for one use of a service, the invitation contains a general app description and App Store link, not your photos, calendar or review results. A successful system share completion grants one use of the selected service. Canceling, copying the link, or simply returning to the app does not grant access. Foliover does not read recipients or verify that someone received the invitation.

07Apple purchases, backups and reports

Apple processes App Store purchases, subscriptions and restores. The app uses StoreKit product and transaction information to determine access. We do not receive your payment-card number or Apple Account password. Apple may provide the developer with sales or subscription reports and diagnostics according to its services and your device choices; relevant reports may be used to maintain the app and resolve issues.

The app has no developer-managed cloud account or cloud synchronization service. iCloud Photos, cloud Files providers, calendar sync and device backups operate according to your system and provider settings. App data may be included in a device backup. The app does not override these services’ retention or security settings.

Apple’s services are governed by Apple’s privacy notices. No separate advertising or analytics service is embedded by Foliover.

08Sharing, support and external pages

When you choose Share, Save to Photos, Save to Files or a printing destination, the output and any visible personal information are delivered to the app, provider, printer or recipient you select. Their policies govern copies they receive. Saving to Photos requests add-only permission when needed. Foliover does not automatically send these files to the developer.

If you email support or make a privacy request, we receive your email address and any name, message, attachments or device details you choose to send. We use this information to respond, investigate your issue, handle your request and keep necessary records. Sending support content is optional. Please send only what is needed and redact private information where possible.

Your email providers handle message delivery and storage. If you open an external policy, support or Apple page, the website provider can receive normal request information such as IP address and browser details. The policy page supplied with Foliover contains no advertising, analytics scripts or tracking cookies. We do not disclose support correspondence for advertising; we may disclose it where required by law or necessary to establish or defend legal rights.

09Retention, deletion and your controls

Local review caches remain until you clear or replace them, the app invalidates them, or its ordinary app storage is removed. Studio exports remain until you delete them or remove that storage. Temporary files remain until cleared by the app, iOS or removal of the app. Preferences remain until changed or ordinary app storage is removed.

We retain support correspondence only for as long as reasonably needed to resolve the request and retain necessary records for legal, security or dispute purposes. We consider whether the issue is still open, whether a record remains needed and any applicable retention requirement. Apple, email providers and export destinations control their own copies and retention.

Deleting the app removes ordinary app storage managed by iOS, but is not a guarantee that Keychain records, device backups, Apple transactions or copies outside the app are erased. We cannot remotely delete local photos or files because we do not hold developer-server copies.

  • You → Clear photo review cache removes local review information without deleting your photos.
  • You → Delete Studio work, or a project’s Delete export action, removes the app’s stored photo and print copies and thumbnails.
  • Timestamp → Settings → Clear temporary files removes Timestamp working files. Edit or clear saved custom labels and notes in the corresponding stamp settings.
  • Use iOS Settings to change Photos, Calendar, Camera or Location permissions. Declining a permission limits the corresponding feature, not access to this policy.
  • Manage shared copies in Photos, Files or the destination you selected, and manage backups through your backup provider. Photos recovery availability is controlled by Apple Photos; calendar deletion has no Foliover recovery service.

Foliover is a general-purpose utility and is not designed to solicit personal information from children. We do not knowingly solicit children’s personal information through support. If a parent or guardian believes a child has supplied such information without appropriate authorization, please contact us so we can address and delete it where required.

For questions, privacy requests or complaints, contact the Foliover developer at wanglingbo0227@126.com. Include “Foliover Privacy” in the subject and describe the request. You do not need to attach private photos or calendar contents to ask a privacy question.

02隐私概要

Foliover 无需注册账号。照片与日历分析、照片编辑、背景处理和水印绘制在设备上完成。App 不将你的图库、日历内容、复核结果或位置历史发送到开发者运营的服务器。

我们不嵌入广告、第三方追踪或统计 SDK，不申请跨 App 追踪权限，不出售个人信息，也不为跨情境行为广告共享个人信息。不使用你的照片、日历或位置训练开发者运营的 AI 模型。

本地处理不代表每个可选操作都离线。购买和可选地址解析由 Apple 提供；系统导入、备份、导出及邮件可能涉及你使用的服务提供者。

03照片、导入与本地分析

开始照片复核时申请图库权限。有限访问仅复核 iOS 根据你的选择提供的项目。分析使用照片或视频及元数据，包括图库标识符、创建和修改日期、尺寸、时长、估计大小、收藏和编辑状态。

App 在本地计算内容指纹、相似分组和质量观察，帮助复核重复、相似或其他可能需要整理的内容。元数据、指纹、分类和检测理由可能缓存于 App 内。这些只是建议；删除前由你选择并确认。

Photo & print 可在本地检测人脸位置与姿态以提供构图建议，并处理人物背景。不识别人、比较身份、建立身份数据库或向我们发送面部测量。分析用于当前编辑，我们不将其保留为生物识别身份档案。

单张导入使用系统 Photos 或 Files 选择器，无需完整图库权限。所选文件可能包含拍摄日期、时区及 GPS 元数据，Timestamp 可读取它们以展示原照片的记录。Apple 或文件提供者可能下载你选定的云端文件，这与本地分析是不同的过程。

04日历整理

开始日历复核时申请权限。在本地读取可访问的事件与日历信息，包括标题、日期、备注、地点、URL 和日历或来源名称，用于识别可能重复或不需要的事项。不向我们上传这些内容，扫描也不会访问事件 URL。

扫描不会自动改动日历。只有你选择并确认、且具备编辑权限的项目才会被更改。日历提供者可能将变更同步到其他设备。只读订阅可能需要在 iOS 设置中处理。Foliover 不提供已删日历事件的 App 内恢复服务。

05相机、时间与可选地点

启用拍摄时申请相机权限，帧与照片在本地处理。静态照片拍摄功能不申请麦克风权限。

实时定位默认关闭。开启后，在功能使用期间通过 iOS 定位权限获取坐标、精度和拍摄时刻用于水印。不申请后台定位，也不运营开发者位置历史服务。可在系统允许时使用近似位置，或选择自定义文字、不显示地点。

选择当前定位后，Foliover 会自动将坐标发送到 Apple 地址解析服务以获取完整地址，不再提供单独的地址查询开关。地址解析成功后停止定位更新与自动地址查询，保存的地点保留至刷新或替换。App 保留原始采样时间，不把保存的地点当作新的 GPS 采样。可以编辑地址文字而不改变坐标，水印会标明编辑来源。导入照片打开地点编辑器后，可以解析该照片已有的坐标，不使用当前 GPS 地点。Apple 按其自身隐私条款处理服务数据。如不希望查询地址，可以选择自定义地点或不显示地点。

Timestamp 导出新的图像副本，不保留原始 GPS 与私密原图 EXIF。但水印中可见的时间、地点、坐标或备注已经成为图像的一部分，分享时仍会保留。水印说明设备时间、照片元数据或你的编辑，不认证真实性或阻止之后的修改。

主动分享 App 换取单项服务一次使用时，分享内容是通用 App 介绍与商店链接，不含照片、日历或复核结果。系统返回分享成功后获得所选服务一次使用；取消、复制链接或仅返回 App 不发放次数。App 不读取接收者，也不验证对方是否收到。
