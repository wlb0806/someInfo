照片制作：常用照片尺寸 隐私政策

更新日期及生效日期：2026 年 10 月 29 日

照片编辑在您的设备上完成。我们不接收您在应用中编辑的照片，不提供照片上传服务器，也不使用广告或跨应用追踪。作品保存在应用本地；Apple 服务、您主动分享的目标和您发送给我们的邮件，按下文说明处理相关信息。

1. 适用范围

本政策适用于 Photo Maker（照片制作：常用照片尺寸，以下简称“本应用”）及其新版照片工作室和可用的经典功能。“我们”指本应用的开发者。功能是否显示或开放，不改变相同数据处理行为所适用的隐私说明。隐私联系方式为 wanglingbo0227@126.com。

2. 照片与本地处理

本应用使用您通过系统照片选择器选取的图片或主动拍摄的照片，提供裁切、尺寸调整、人像定位、背景替换、文件压缩、JPEG／PNG 导出和多张照片排版等功能。图片处理通过设备上的 Apple 系统框架完成，不发送至我们的服务器或远程 AI 服务，也不用于训练我们的模型。

本应用不要求注册账户，不读取通讯录或请求定位权限，不会主动扫描或上传整个照片图库。您选择的照片本身可能包含人像、文字或其他个人信息，这些内容按本政策所述在本地处理。

3. 人脸与人物分割信息

为了辅助取景、裁切、背景替换以及可用的照片检查功能，本应用可能在设备上检测人脸位置、面部关键点、姿态、图像质量或人物轮廓。检测结果用于当前照片的处理，不用于辨认个人身份、跨照片身份比对或建立人脸身份数据库。

这些检测结果和人物分割遮罩仅在处理期间保留于内存中，不作为独立的人脸特征档案持久保存，也不传给我们或第三方。保存的作品仍可能含有人像；删除作品的方式见第 9 节。

4. 作品、预设与临时文件

新版照片工作室在生成可导出的成品并通过导出权限检查后，会将成品及缩略图存入本机应用的“作品”列表。该步骤发生在系统相册保存或分享完成之前，因此即使之后拒绝相册权限、保存失败或取消分享，作品也可能已保留。多页排版会分别保存各页。

本地作品记录还包含创建时间、尺寸、分辨率、文件格式、文件大小、本地标识符，以及用于避免重复保存的文件摘要值。这些记录不上传给我们。自定义尺寸预设和最近输入的尺寸等偏好也保存在本地。

编辑、压缩和分享可能生成临时图片文件。应用会在相关流程结束时尝试清理部分临时文件，其余临时文件由系统缓存机制管理；我们不承诺每个文件都会立即删除。

5. 相机与照片权限

  • 相机：仅在您主动选择拍照时使用；拒绝后仍可选择已有照片。
  • 选择照片：通过系统照片选择器读取您选中的图片，无需为此授予整个图库的读取权限。若所选照片由 iCloud 管理，其获取过程由系统按您的设置处理。
  • 保存照片：保存成品时请求向系统相册添加照片的权限。拒绝该权限会影响保存到相册，但仍可使用系统分享或存储到文件等可用方式。

您可在 iOS“设置”中查看或更改已授予的权限。拒绝或撤回权限不会自动删除已生成的作品或已导出的文件。

6. 购买与订阅

月度订阅、永久买断、付款及恢复购买通过 Apple App Store 和 StoreKit 处理。本应用读取 Apple 提供并验证的商品与交易信息，例如商品标识、交易标识、有效期、续订状态、退款或撤销状态及适用的付款宽限期，用于判断导出权限、显示购买状态和恢复权益。

我们没有接收上述交易记录的自建验证服务器。本应用不会获取您的 Apple 账户密码或完整支付卡信息。Apple 独立处理付款、账户和交易记录；相关信息见 Apple 隐私政策。删除本应用或作品不会自动取消订阅，也不会删除 Apple 保存的购买记录。

7. 分享、备份与外部页面

当您通过系统分享菜单将图片交给其他应用、联系人或文件存储服务时，所选目标会接收您分享的文件，并按其自身规则处理。我们不会在您未选择分享目标时，将作品主动发送给这些服务。

本应用没有自行实现作品云同步。根据您的设备设置，本地应用数据可能纳入 iCloud 或电脑备份，另存到系统相册的图片也可能通过 iCloud 照片同步。上述行为由 Apple、操作系统及您选用的服务控制，并不表示我们能够读取这些备份。备份范围见 Apple 的 iCloud 备份说明。

打开在线隐私政策、使用条款、App Store 或其他参考链接时，需要连接相应网站。隐私政策网页由 GitHub Pages 托管，托管服务可能处理 IP 地址、浏览器信息和访问日志以提供服务；见 GitHub 隐私声明。本政策页面不添加广告、统计脚本或追踪 Cookie。

8. 分析、广告与主动联系

本应用不集成第三方广告或行为分析 SDK，不使用广告标识符，不进行跨应用追踪，也不出售您的个人信息。Apple 可能依据您的设备和账户设置处理系统诊断或 App Store 相关信息，具体以 Apple 的说明为准。

如果您主动发送支持邮件，我们会收到您的邮箱地址、邮件内容及您附带的文件，仅用于回复、排查问题和处理您的请求。邮件由通信服务提供商传输和存储。请仅提供解决问题所必需的信息，无需发送照片原件、身份证件号码或付款凭据中的敏感内容。支持往来仅保留处理请求所需的时间，或适用法律要求的期限。

9. 保存期限、删除与您的选择

  • 应用内作品与预设：保存在设备上，供您后续使用；您可在应用内删除相应项目。删除作品会移除应用管理的成品副本、缩略图和列表记录，不会删除原始照片、相册副本或已分享的副本。
  • 相册及文件副本：请在“照片”“文件”或接收方服务中分别管理和删除；这些服务可能有“最近删除”等保留机制。
  • 应用数据：在系统设置中选择“删除 App”会删除该设备上的应用本地数据；“卸载 App”可能保留文稿与数据。系统备份或其他设备的副本需另行管理，恢复备份可能恢复其中包含的数据。
  • 购买记录：由 Apple 按其政策保存和管理。应用内删除数据不会移除 Apple 的交易记录。

我们无法远程访问或代您删除仅存在于设备上的照片。对于您主动提供给我们的支持资料，您可通过下方邮箱提出访问、更正或删除等适用的隐私请求；我们会在适用法律允许和要求的范围内处理。

10. 安全

本地数据保存在应用沙盒中，受 iOS 的访问控制及设备安全机制保护。请妥善设置设备密码，并谨慎选择分享对象和备份服务。任何存储或传输方式都无法保证绝对安全。

11. 未成年人隐私

本应用不要求提供年龄信息，也不以收集儿童个人信息为目的。未成年人应在监护人指导下使用照片、分享和购买功能。如果您认为儿童通过支持邮件向我们提供了不必要的个人信息，请联系我们处理。

当功能或数据处理方式改变时，我们会更新本政策，并标注新的更新及生效日期；如适用法律要求，将采取相应的告知或同意措施。

Photo Maker: Standard Sizes Privacy Policy

Updated and effective: October 29, 2026

Photo editing takes place on your device. We do not receive the photos you edit in the App, operate a photo-upload server, or use advertising or cross-app tracking. Creations are stored locally. Apple services, destinations you choose for sharing, and emails you send us handle information as described below.

1. Scope

This policy applies to Photo Maker: Standard Sizes (the “App”), including its photo studio and available classic features. “We” means the App’s developer. The same privacy practices apply whether a feature is currently visible or available. Our privacy contact is wanglingbo0227@126.com.

2. Photos and on-device processing

The App uses images you select through the system photo picker or choose to capture with the camera for cropping, resizing, portrait positioning, background replacement, compression, JPEG/PNG export, and multi-photo print layouts. Processing uses Apple frameworks on your device. Photos are not sent to our servers or remote AI services, or used to train our models.

The App does not require an account, read contacts, or request location access. It does not proactively scan or upload your entire photo library. Selected photos may themselves contain faces, text, or other personal information, which is processed locally as described here.

3. Face and person-segmentation information

To assist framing, cropping, background replacement, and available photo checks, the App may detect face locations, facial landmarks, pose, image quality, or person outlines on your device. These results are used to process the current photo, not to identify people, match identities across photos, or build a face-identity database.

Detection results and segmentation masks are held in memory during processing. They are not persistently stored as separate face-feature profiles or sent to us or third parties. Saved creations can still contain portraits; see section 9 for deletion options.

4. Creations, preferences, and temporary files

After generating an exportable result and checking export access, the photo studio stores the finished image and a thumbnail in the App’s local Creations list. This happens before saving to Photos or completing a share. A creation may therefore remain even if you subsequently deny Photos permission, saving fails, or you cancel sharing. Multi-page layouts are saved as separate pages.

Local records include creation time, dimensions, resolution, format, file size, a local identifier, and a file digest used to avoid duplicates. These records are not uploaded to us. Custom size presets and preferences such as recently entered dimensions are also stored locally.

Editing, compression, and sharing may create temporary image files. The App attempts to clean up some temporary files when the relevant flow ends; other temporary files are managed through system cache mechanisms. We do not promise immediate deletion of every temporary file.

5. Camera and Photos permissions

  • Camera: used when you choose to take a photo. You can still select existing images if you decline camera access.
  • Selecting photos: the system photo picker provides the images you select without requiring full-library read permission for this purpose. If a selected photo is managed by iCloud, the system retrieves it according to your settings.
  • Saving photos: the App requests permission to add finished images to Photos. If you decline, saving to Photos is unavailable, but other available options include system sharing or saving to Files.

You can review or change granted permissions in iOS Settings. Declining or withdrawing permission does not automatically delete creations or exported files.

6. Purchases and subscriptions

Monthly subscriptions, lifetime purchases, payments, and purchase restoration use Apple’s App Store and StoreKit. The App reads and verifies product and transaction information supplied by Apple, such as product and transaction identifiers, expiration dates, renewal status, refunds or revocations, and applicable billing grace periods. This information determines export access, displays purchase status, and restores entitlements.

We do not operate a verification server receiving these transaction records. The App does not obtain your Apple Account password or full payment-card details. Apple independently handles payment, account, and transaction records under the Apple Privacy Policy. Deleting the App or creations does not cancel a subscription or delete Apple’s purchase records.

7. Sharing, backups, and external pages

When you share an image with another app, contact, or file-storage service through the system share sheet, that destination receives the files you choose to share and handles them under its own rules. We do not proactively send creations to those services without your choosing a sharing destination.

The App does not implement its own cloud sync for creations. Depending on your settings, local app data may be included in iCloud or computer backups, and images saved to Photos may sync through iCloud Photos. Apple, the operating system, and your selected services control these processes; they do not give us access to your backups. See Apple’s iCloud backup explanation.

Opening the online privacy policy, terms, App Store, or other reference links connects to the relevant website. This privacy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process IP addresses, browser information, and access logs to provide its service; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. This policy page adds no advertising, analytics scripts, or tracking cookies.

8. Analytics, advertising, and contacting us

The App includes no third-party advertising or behavioral analytics SDKs, does not use advertising identifiers, does not track you across apps, and does not sell your personal information. Apple may process system diagnostics or App Store information according to your device and account settings and its own disclosures.

If you email support, we receive your address, message, and any attachments to respond, troubleshoot, and handle your request. Communications providers transmit and store email. Please provide only what is necessary; you do not need to send original photos, identity-document numbers, or sensitive payment details. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle your request or for a period required by applicable law.

9. Retention, deletion, and your choices

  • Creations and presets: stored on your device for future use; you can delete individual items in the App. Deleting a creation removes its App-managed image copy, thumbnail, and list record, not original photos, Photos copies, or previously shared copies.
  • Photos and file copies: manage these separately in Photos, Files, or the recipient service. Those services may have retention features such as Recently Deleted.
  • App data: choosing Delete App in system settings removes local app data on that device. Offloading the App may keep documents and data. Manage system backups and copies on other devices separately; restoring a backup may restore data it contains.
  • Purchase records: retained and managed by Apple under its policies. Deleting local data does not remove Apple’s transaction records.

We cannot remotely access or delete photos stored only on your device. For support information you voluntarily provide us, contact the email below to request access, correction, deletion, or exercise other applicable privacy rights. We handle requests as permitted and required by applicable law.

10. Security

Local data is stored in the App’s sandbox and protected by iOS access controls and device security mechanisms. Protect your device with a passcode and choose sharing recipients and backup services carefully. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

11. Children’s privacy

The App does not request age information or aim to collect children’s personal information. Minors should use photo, sharing, and purchase features with a parent or guardian’s guidance. Contact us if you believe a child has provided unnecessary personal information through support email.

12. Policy changes and contact

When features or data practices change, we will update this policy and its updated/effective date, and provide notice or obtain consent where applicable law requires it.

For privacy questions or requests, contact the developer at wanglingbo0227@126.com.
