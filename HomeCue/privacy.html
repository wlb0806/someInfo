Privacy at HomeCue

HomeCue keeps your item details, receipts, return dates, warranties, and care records under your control.

Support简体中文

Effective date: October 22, 2026

This policy describes HomeCue 2.0. If you use an earlier version with optional iCloud backup, see “Earlier versions” below.

Your records, under your control: HomeCue does not require a developer account and has no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. Item details, receipts, documents, and care records are stored locally. Text recognition runs on your device. HomeCue 2.0 does not offer app-managed iCloud sync. Apple handles optional in-app purchases.

Information you choose to store

HomeCue stores the information you enter or import: item names and locations, brand and model details, serial numbers, mileage, maintenance plans and history, costs, service providers, notes, images, receipts, merchant and purchase details, return deadlines and status, warranty dates, documents, and preferences. Receipts and documents may contain personal information. This content is kept in the app’s local storage and is not uploaded to a developer-operated server.

Camera, selected photos, documents, and OCR

Camera capture, photo selection, document importing, and scanning are used when you choose those features. Selected content is copied into the app as needed for your records. Text recognition runs on your device; HomeCue does not send photos, scans, or recognized text to an external OCR or AI service. Camera and photo-related access is optional. You can use the built-in illustrations without providing photos, and manage permissions in iOS Settings.

In-app purchases and restoration

The optional full unlock is a one-time, non-consumable purchase, not a subscription. Apple processes payment and purchase restoration through the App Store under its own terms and privacy policy. HomeCue uses StoreKit transaction verification to determine your entitlement and stores the verified unlock status locally. The developer does not receive your payment-card details or operate a purchase-account server. Restoring a purchase restores access to the unlock; it does not restore your item records, receipts, or local backup files.

Local reminders

With your permission, HomeCue schedules notifications locally through iOS for date-based care and return reminders. Reminder content may include item names and dates and may appear on your lock screen according to your system settings. HomeCue does not use a developer-operated remote notification service. You can change notification permissions in iOS Settings.

Backups, PDFs, and sharing

When you export a backup, care record, or purchase proof, HomeCue creates the requested file and lets you choose a destination using the system share interface. Your selected file may contain personal records and attachments. HomeCue does not add password protection or its own encryption to exported backup files. Save and share them only with destinations you trust. Any cloud storage, recipient, or other service you choose handles the shared copy under its own terms and privacy policy. HomeCue cannot delete copies outside the app.

Device backups and earlier versions

HomeCue 2.0 does not provide cross-device iCloud sync or a developer-hosted cloud backup. Depending on your device settings, iOS or computer backups may include app data; those system backups are managed by you and Apple, separately from HomeCue’s export feature.

Earlier versions: Some earlier HomeCue releases offered optional iCloud backup. If you enabled that feature, copies may remain in your private Apple iCloud storage. The developer cannot view that private content. You can manage those copies in your Apple Account’s iCloud storage settings. The current app does not automatically import or synchronize those earlier cloud records.

Developer collection and external services

HomeCue does not collect your item records into a developer-controlled system, sell personal information, track you across apps or websites, or include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

If you open a privacy, support, or other external link, your browser connects to that website. This policy and the support page are hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Opening a website does not attach your HomeCue records. If you email support, your email address and the information you choose to include are used to respond. Please do not send private receipts or other sensitive files unless necessary for your request.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

Local records remain until you remove them or delete the app. You can delete items or documents from within HomeCue; deleting an item does not automatically delete an independently stored receipt. Deleting the app removes its local app container, while offloading the app may preserve data under iOS settings. Exported files, shared copies, and system or earlier iCloud backups must be managed separately at their destinations. You can stop optional camera or notification access in iOS Settings. There is no HomeCue developer account to close.

Children and policy changes

HomeCue is a general-audience utility and is not designed to collect children’s information through a developer-operated service. Material changes to this policy will be posted at this same address with an updated effective date.
