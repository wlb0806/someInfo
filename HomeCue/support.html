Privacy简体中文

How do I add maintenance?

Open Assets, choose an asset, then tap Add Maintenance. Select a template or create a custom task and choose a date, mileage, or whichever comes first.

Where are my records stored?

They are stored locally on your device. If you explicitly turn on iCloud Sync in Settings, HomeCue also keeps a backup in your private iCloud container.

Why did a reminder not appear?

Check notification access in iOS Settings, then confirm that the task has a due date and reminders are permitted for HomeCue. Mileage-only tasks require you to update the asset’s current mileage.

How do I back up or move my data?

Open Settings and use Export Data to create a HomeCue backup. You can save it to Files or another destination using the iOS share sheet. Optional iCloud Sync can also maintain a private cloud backup.

How do I remove my data?

Delete individual documents in the asset detail screen. Deleting HomeCue removes its local app container. If iCloud Sync was enabled, manage the cloud copy in your Apple Account’s iCloud storage settings.

简体中文帮助

添加维护：打开“资产”，选择具体资产，点击“添加维护”。

数据位置：数据默认保存在设备本地；如在“设置”中主动开启 iCloud 同步，备份会保存到你的 iCloud 私有容器。

提醒未出现：请检查 iOS 通知权限、任务到期日期；仅按里程提醒的任务还需要更新资产当前里程。

备份数据：在“设置”中点击“导出数据”，通过系统分享面板保存备份。
