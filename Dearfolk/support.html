English简体中文Privacy Policy

English

How do I add someone?

Open People, tap +, then add their name, relationship, closeness, and any details you want.

How do private profiles work?

Set a private-record password in Settings, then mark selected profiles as private. They stay hidden from the main view until you reveal them with that password. This is an in-app visibility control, not full file encryption.

Can I use a photo or Live Photo as the cover?

Yes. Open a person, browse their media, and choose a photo or Live Photo as the profile cover. Videos can remain in the media collection but cannot be moving covers.

How do reminders and Apple Calendar work?

Add a birthday or important date, then enable a local reminder. Adding the date to Apple Calendar is a separate action and happens only with your permission.

Where is my data stored?

Version 1.0 stores records and imported media locally on your iPhone. There is no Dearfolk account or developer-operated sync server, and iCloud sync is not enabled in this version.

How do I delete data?

Delete an individual profile inside Dearfolk to remove its record and associated media. Deleting Dearfolk removes its local app data, subject to Apple’s normal device backup behavior.

简体中文

怎样添加人物？

打开“人物”，点击 +，然后填写姓名、关系、亲密度以及你想记录的其他资料。

隐私人物怎样使用？

先在设置中设置隐私档案密码，再把指定人物设为隐私。人物会从主界面隐藏，输入密码后才能显示。这是应用内的可见性控制，并非完整的文件加密。

可以把照片或实况照片设为封面吗？

可以。打开人物的媒体浏览器，选择一张照片或实况照片设为人物封面。视频可以保存在人物媒体中，但不能作为动态封面。

提醒与 Apple 日历怎样工作？

添加生日或重要日子后，可以启用本机提醒。写入 Apple 日历是独立操作，只会在你主动选择和授权后发生。

数据保存在哪里？

1.0 版本的人物记录与导入媒体保存在你的 iPhone 本机。珍念没有账号和开发者运营的同步服务器，当前版本也未启用 iCloud 同步。

怎样删除数据？

在珍念内删除单个人物，会移除其记录与关联媒体。删除应用会移除本地数据，但 Apple 管理的设备备份可能仍按系统规则保留。
