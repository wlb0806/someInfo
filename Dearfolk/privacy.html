Dearfolk Privacy Policy

珍念隐私政策 · Effective September 18, 2026

Dearfolk is a local-first people journal. Your records stay in the app’s private storage on your iPhone unless you explicitly use an Apple system feature to share or export something.

English简体中文Support

English

Information you add

You may add names, relationship tags, birthdays and other dates, contact details, interests, notes, custom fields, moments, photos, videos, Live Photos, attention history, and a private-profile status. This content is stored locally in the app’s private container on your device.

Photos and camera

Dearfolk accesses photos, videos, Live Photos, or the camera only after you choose the related action and grant Apple’s permission. Imported media is copied into the app’s local storage.

Notifications and calendar

Reminder notifications are scheduled on your device. Dearfolk writes an event to Apple Calendar only when you explicitly choose to do so and grant calendar access.

Private profiles

You can hide selected profiles behind a private-record password. The password verifier is stored in Apple Keychain. This feature controls visibility inside Dearfolk; it is not full device or file encryption. Protect your iPhone with a strong passcode and keep iOS up to date.

Collection, tracking, and sharing

Dearfolk 1.0 requires no account and sends no personal content to a developer-operated server. We do not collect analytics, show ads, track you, sell personal information, or share your content with third parties. iCloud synchronization is not enabled in version 1.0.

Deletion and retention

Deleting a profile removes its stored record and associated media from the app. Deleting the app removes its local app data, subject to normal device backup behavior managed by Apple.

Children and changes

Dearfolk is a general-audience organizational tool and is not directed at children under 13. We may update this policy when the app changes; the effective date above will be revised.

简体中文

你添加的信息

你可以添加姓名、关系标签、生日和其他日期、联系方式、兴趣、备注、自定义字段、相处动态、照片、视频、实况照片、本机关注记录以及隐私人物状态。这些内容保存在你设备上本应用的私有存储空间中。

照片与相机

只有当你主动选择相关功能并授予 Apple 系统权限后，珍念才会访问照片、视频、实况照片或相机。导入的媒体会复制到应用的本地存储中。

通知与日历

提醒通知在你的设备上安排。只有当你主动选择并授予日历权限时，珍念才会向 Apple 日历写入事件。

隐私人物

你可以用隐私档案密码隐藏指定人物。密码校验信息保存在 Apple 钥匙串中。此功能用于控制人物在珍念内的可见性，不等同于设备或文件的完整加密。请为 iPhone 设置可靠的设备密码并及时更新 iOS。

收集、跟踪与共享

珍念 1.0 无需账号，不会把个人内容发送到开发者运营的服务器。我们不收集分析数据、不展示广告、不跟踪用户、不出售个人信息，也不会与第三方共享你的内容。1.0 版本暂未启用 iCloud 同步。

删除与保留

删除人物会从应用中移除其记录和关联媒体。删除应用会移除应用的本地数据，但 Apple 管理的设备备份可能仍按系统规则保留。

儿童与政策变更

珍念是面向一般用户的整理工具，并非专门面向 13 岁以下儿童。应用发生变化时，我们可能更新本政策，并同步修改生效日期。
