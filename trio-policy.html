Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Trio is designed to respect your privacy. In short: the developer does not collect, store, or share any personal information about you. Trio has no servers of its own, and all core gameplay works offline. If iCloud is available, Apple can privately sync your game progress between your devices.

What we don’t do

  • No accounts, logins, or sign-ups.
  • No analytics, tracking, or third-party SDKs.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No collection of your name, email, contacts, or location.
  • Trio never sends game data to the developer, advertisers, analytics services, or other third parties. Puzzles ship inside the app.

Game data

  • Your progress — including solved puzzles, solve times, hint usage, and streaks — is stored on your device, along with settings such as sound and haptics.
  • When you are signed in to iCloud and iCloud is available, progress may also be stored in your private Apple iCloud key-value store so it can sync between your devices.
  • The developer cannot read or access your local or private iCloud progress. iCloud storage and transmission are handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy and terms.
  • The game remains playable if iCloud is disabled or unavailable. Deleting Trio removes its local data; a synced copy may remain in your iCloud account according to your Apple settings.
  • If you use Apple’s device backup, local game data may also be included in your own backup. We cannot access that backup.

Purchases

“Trio Pro” is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple. Apple processes the payment; we never receive or see your payment details. Whether you own Trio Pro is verified through Apple’s App Store.

Sharing your results

When you tap Share, Trio creates a result-card image and a short summary containing your puzzle number and results, then hands them — together with an App Store link — to the standard iOS share sheet. You choose where they go, and nothing is sent unless you send it.

Children

Trio is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date.
