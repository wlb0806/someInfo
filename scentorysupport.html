How does iCloud sync work?

Scentory saves changes on your device first and uses Apple CloudKit to synchronize them to your private iCloud database. Sign in to the same Apple Account and enable iCloud Drive on each device. You can check the current status and latest update in Insights.

Can I use Scentory offline?

Yes. You can browse and edit your wardrobe without a connection. Changes remain on the device and synchronize when iCloud becomes available again.

What happens to my existing collection?

Updating to Scentory 2.0 keeps the existing on-device database in place. When iCloud is available, Scentory uploads that collection to your private iCloud database.

How do I restore Scentory Pro?

Open the Scentory Pro screen and choose Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple Account used for the original purchase. Subscription management is available in Apple Account settings.

How do I remove my data?

Delete individual fragrances in Scentory before uninstalling the app so those deletions can synchronize. Data stored in iCloud is managed through your Apple Account and Apple's iCloud tools.
