CalendarKiller 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 7 月 5 日

CalendarKiller 由个人开发者提供。本政策说明应用如何处理与隐私相关的信息。

我们不收集个人数据

CalendarKiller 不要求创建账号，不接入第三方广告 SDK，不接入第三方统计分析 SDK，也不会请求跨 App 或跨网站追踪权限。

日历权限的用途

CalendarKiller 会请求访问你的日历，用于在设备本机扫描日历事件，识别疑似垃圾事件、重复事件和订阅日历相关项目。应用可能读取事件标题、备注、链接、地点、日期、日历名称和日历类型，以完成分类和展示。

本机处理

CalendarKiller 的日历扫描和分类在你的设备上完成。应用本身不会把你的日历数据上传到开发者服务器。

删除操作

CalendarKiller 只会删除你在应用中选择并确认的可编辑日历事件。只读日历或无法修改的事件会被跳过。

购买信息

CalendarKiller Pro 的购买和订阅由 Apple App Store 和 StoreKit 处理。开发者不会在应用内收集你的支付卡号或 Apple ID 密码。

儿童隐私

CalendarKiller 面向普通效率工具使用场景，并不主动收集儿童个人信息。

政策更新

如果应用功能或数据处理方式发生变化，本隐私政策可能会更新。更新后的版本会发布在本页面。

联系与支持

如需支持，请访问 CalendarKiller 支持页面，或通过 App Store 产品页显示的开发者联系方式联系开发者。

CalendarKiller Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 5, 2026

CalendarKiller is provided by an individual developer. This policy explains how the app handles privacy-related information.

We Do Not Collect Personal Data

CalendarKiller does not require an account, does not include third-party advertising SDKs, does not include third-party analytics SDKs, and does not request permission to track you across apps or websites.

Calendar Permission

CalendarKiller requests access to your calendar so it can scan events on your device and identify suspicious events, duplicate events, and subscribed-calendar items. The app may read event titles, notes, URLs, locations, dates, calendar names, and calendar types to classify and display results.

On-Device Processing

Calendar scanning and classification are performed on your device. The app itself does not upload your calendar data to a developer server.

Deletion

CalendarKiller only deletes editable calendar events that you select and confirm in the app. Read-only or non-editable events are skipped.

Purchases

CalendarKiller Pro purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple App Store and StoreKit. The developer does not collect your payment card number or Apple ID password inside the app.

Children's Privacy

CalendarKiller is intended as a general productivity tool and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If app features or data practices change, this privacy policy may be updated. The updated version will be posted on this page.

Contact and Support

For support, please visit the CalendarKiller support page or contact the developer through the contact information shown on the App Store product page.
