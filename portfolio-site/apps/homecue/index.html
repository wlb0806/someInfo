Maintenance and receipt tracking questions

Can I track vehicle maintenance by mileage?

Yes. Use a mileage threshold or pair it with a date. Update the recorded vehicle mileage so HomeCue can show how close you are to the next service.

Can I link one receipt to multiple items?

Yes. A receipt can be linked to several items, and returns can be tracked item by item.

Does HomeCue sync to other devices?

HomeCue stores records locally. You can export and restore a backup. Check your App Store for current pricing and unlock availability.
