Frequently Asked Questions

Why am I not receiving reminders?

Open Settings → Notifications → Daily Quest and make sure notifications are allowed. Also confirm that the task reminder is enabled and its time is correct. Reminders are local and do not require a network connection.

Where is my data stored?

Your tasks, completion records, reminder settings, and progress stay on your device. No account is required, and this content is not uploaded to servers operated by us.

Can I recover data after deleting the app?

Deleting the app may also delete its local data. Because we do not keep a server copy, we cannot restore it for you. Please make sure you no longer need your data before removing the app.

What should I include in a support request?

Please describe the steps that caused the issue and include your app version, iOS/iPadOS version, and device model. Do not send task contents or other sensitive information.
