Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 3, 2026

Daily Quest works without an account, and your data stays on your device. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

Daily Quest does not require registration or sign-in. We do not collect names, email addresses, location data, device identifiers, usage data, or other personal information. The app contains no advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Your tasks, completion records, reminder settings, and progress are stored locally on your device. This information is not uploaded to servers operated by us. Deleting the app may also delete this local data, so please make sure you no longer need it before removing the app.

3. Notifications

If you grant notification permission, the app schedules reminders locally on your device. Notification content is generated on your device and is not sent to our servers. You can disable notifications at any time in iOS or iPadOS Settings.

4. Sharing

When you choose to share content through the system share sheet, the content is sent to the app or service you select. Any processing performed by that third party is governed by its own privacy policy.

5. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the app requires no account and stores its data locally, we do not receive children's personal data.

6. Changes to This Policy

If the app's features or data practices change, we will update this page and revise the effective date. We will provide appropriate notice of any material changes.
